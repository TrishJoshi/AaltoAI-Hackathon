# EU AI Act use standard (synthetic excerpt, for demo)

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act), Arts. 6, 10, 13, and 14. Second
reusable standard beside GDPR: risk class, human oversight, logging, and a
model card. Governance keeps final say.

1. Risk classification
   Each production AI system must record its AI Act risk class: prohibited,
   high-risk, limited, or minimal. Unclassified production AI is not permitted.
   Systems that do not use AI may record this as not applicable.

2. Human oversight
   High-risk AI systems that produce decisions or recommendations affecting
   students, staff, or research subjects must keep a named human in the loop.
   Fully automated high-impact decisions without a reviewer are not permitted.
   Systems that are not high-risk may record this as not applicable.

3. Logging of high-impact outputs
   Privileged or high-impact AI outputs (admissions, grading, hiring, or
   access-control recommendations) must be logged for at least 90 days.
   Unlogged high-impact outputs are not permitted.

4. Transparency
   Production AI features must publish a short model card: purpose, model
   family, and known limitations. Undocumented production models are not
   allowed. Systems without production AI may record this as not applicable.
