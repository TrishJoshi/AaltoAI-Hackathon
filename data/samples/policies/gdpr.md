# GDPR processing standard (synthetic excerpt, for demo)

Source: Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Arts. 5, 6, 28, 30, and 44–46. A data
protection officer loads this once. Closed checks become the reusable source
of truth for every product team. Governance keeps final say.

1. International transfers
   Personal data of EU persons must not be transferred to a third country unless
   the destination is the EU, the EEA, or a country covered by an adequacy
   decision. Transfers to the United States without an approved transfer tool
   are not permitted for production systems that process personal data.

2. Lawful basis
   Each processing activity must record a lawful basis under Article 6.
   Accepted bases are consent, contract, legitimate interest, and legal
   obligation. Processing personal data with no recorded basis is not permitted.

3. Processor agreements
   Where a processor handles personal data, an Article 28 data processing
   agreement must be signed with that processor. Projects that do not use a
   processor may record this requirement as not applicable.

4. Storage limitation
   Personal data must not be kept longer than a documented, finite retention
   period. Indefinite retention of CRM extracts or event logs is not permitted.

5. Record of processing
   The processing activity must be listed in the organisation’s record of
   processing activities (Art. 30). A project that cannot confirm the RoPA row
   is not yet complete.
